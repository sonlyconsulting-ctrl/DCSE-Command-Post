WEEK 1: MINDSET & EMOTIONAL CLARITY

Day 1 | Understanding Mindsets

Theme Overview: Mindsets shape how you perceive and act. Identifying subconscious mindsets (e.g., cynical, perfectionist) enables conscious change.

Mini-Framework: Mindset Audit: Identify your mindset, its impact, and an alternative.

🧠 Thinker's Tip: Your mindset isn’t fixed; you can choose a new lens.

Visual Aid: Describe a 'mindset' (e.g., cynical, perfectionist, growth-oriented) that often shapes your decisions in a particular area of your life. Outline its 'Impact' on your actions and propose an 'Alternative Mindset' you'd like to adopt. Reflect on how consciously choosing a new 'lens' can transform your approach. Response Area: Describe your visual aid (up to 100 words)

💡 Prompt 1: Write about a mindset (e.g., cynical, optimistic) that shapes your decisions.

Response Area: Up to 25 words

✏️ Prompt 2: Using Mindset Audit, describe how this mindset affects your actions. Propose an alternative mindset.

Response Area: Between 50–75 words

🎯 Prompt 3: How might this mindset stem from past experiences or fears?

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

🔄 Bonus: Reflect on: “The mind is everything. What you think, you become.” —Buddha. How does this relate to mindsets?

Response Area: 10-word summary + 100-word explanation

Dig Deeper: Explain your mindset to a friend to spark discussion.

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

Day 2 | Affective Labeling

Theme Overview: Naming emotions (affective labeling) clarifies feelings, enabling rational decisions.

Mini-Framework: Emotion Map: Name your emotion, describe its trigger, and assess its impact.

🧠 Thinker's Tip: Labeling emotions reduces their control over your choices.

Visual Aid: Recall a recent situation where a strong emotion influenced a decision. Describe your 'Emotion Map': name the 'Emotion', identify its 'Trigger', and assess its 'Impact' on your thinking. Reflect on how simply labeling emotions can reduce their control and lead to more rational choices. Response Area: Describe your visual aid (up to 100 words)

💡 Prompt 1: Write about a recent strong emotion that influenced a decision.

Response Area: Up to 25 words

✏️ Prompt 2: Using Emotion Map, name the emotion, its trigger, and its impact on your thinking.

Response Area: Between 50–75 words

🎯 Prompt 3: How can labeling this emotion improve your future decisions?

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

🔄 Bonus: Practice affective labeling for a current situation. Explain its effect.

Response Area: Labeling + 100-word explanation

Dig Deeper: Explain affective labeling to a 10-year-old using a story.

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

Day 3 | Cognitive Scripts

Theme Overview: Cognitive scripts (e.g., sequel, crowd-pleaser) are unconscious patterns driving decisions. Recognizing them fosters conscious choice.

Mini-Framework: Script Analysis: Identify your script, its origin, and a new script.

🧠 Thinker's Tip: Scripts aren’t destiny; rewrite them for empowerment.

Visual Aid: Identify a 'recurring decision pattern' in your life that feels like an unconscious 'Cognitive Script' (e.g., always saying yes, procrastinating). Describe its 'Origin' (where it might have come from) and then 'Rewrite' a new, empowering 'Script' for a specific situation. Reflect on how consciously rewriting these patterns fosters intentional choice. Response Area: Describe your visual aid (up to 100 words)

💡 Prompt 1: Write about a recurring decision pattern in your life.

Response Area: Up to 25 words

✏️ Prompt 2: Using Script Analysis, identify the script’s origin and propose a new script.

Response Area: Between 50–75 words

🎯 Prompt 3: How does this script limit or empower your choices?

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

🔄 Bonus: Rewrite your script for a specific decision. Explain its potential.

Response Area: New script + 100-word explanation

Dig Deeper: Compare your script with one from a year ago.

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

Day 4 | Decision Clarity

Theme Overview: Conscious decisions balance logic, emotion, and purpose. Clarity comes from aligning choices with values.

Mini-Framework: Triple Check (Head, Heart, Hand): Check logic (head), emotion (heart), and action (hand).

🧠 Thinker's Tip: Misaligned decisions create inner conflict; align them with purpose.

Visual Aid: Think of a recent decision you're unsure about. Describe how you would apply the 'Head-Heart-Hand' framework: what does your 'Head' (logic) suggest, what does your 'Heart' (emotion/intuition) feel, and what 'Action' (hand) aligns with your purpose? Reflect on how aligning these three aspects brings clarity and reduces inner conflict. Response Area: Describe your visual aid (up to 100 words)

💡 Prompt 1: Write about a recent decision you’re unsure about.

Response Area: Up to 25 words

✏️ Prompt 2: Using Triple Check (Head, Heart, Hand), analyze logic, emotion, and action. Are they aligned?

Response Area: Between 50–75 words

🎯 Prompt 3: How can you align this decision with your core values?

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

🔄 Bonus: Reflect on: “The heart has its reasons which reason knows not.” —Pascal. How does this relate to decision clarity?

Response Area: 10-word summary + 100-word explanation

Dig Deeper: Explain decision clarity to a colleague using a workplace example.

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

Day 5 | Self-Anthropology

Theme Overview: Self-anthropology is observing your energy, emotions, and executive function to understand your inner world.

Mini-Framework: Observation Log: Track your energy, emotions, and decisions daily.

🧠 Thinker's Tip: Observing yourself like an anthropologist reveals hidden patterns.

Visual Aid: Describe a typical day, focusing on your 'Observation Log': track your fluctuating 'Energy' levels, the 'Emotions' you experience, and key 'Decisions' you make. Reflect on what patterns emerge and how observing yourself like an anthropologist can reveal hidden insights for better decision-making. Response Area: Describe your visual aid (up to 100 words)

💡 Prompt 1: Write about a recent day when your energy or emotions shifted noticeably.

Response Area: Up to 25 words

✏️ Prompt 2: Using Observation Log, track energy, emotions, and decisions for that day. What patterns emerge?

Response Area: Between 50–75 words

🎯 Prompt 3: How can self-observation improve your decision-making?

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

🔄 Bonus: Log a current day’s energy and emotions. Explain insights.

Response Area: Log + 100-word explanation

Dig Deeper: Share your self-observation insights with a trusted friend.

Response Area: Between 50–100 words

WEEK 1 CHECKPOINT

Reflect on your progress in mindset and emotional clarity. Which mini-framework deepened your awareness most? What surprised you about your inner world?

Response Area: Between 100–150 words

Day 7 | Managing Emotional Triggers

Theme Overview: Emotional triggers can derail purposeful decisions. Managing them fosters clarity.

Mini-Framework: Trigger Response: Identify trigger, emotion, and conscious response.

Visual Aid: Recall a recent 'Emotional Trigger' you experienced. Describe your 'Trigger Response': identify the 'Trigger' itself, the 'Emotion' it evoked, and a 'Conscious Response' you could choose instead of a reactive one. Reflect on how pausing before responding to triggers fosters greater clarity and control. Response Area: Describe your visual aid (up to 100 words)

Response Area: Up to 25 words

✏️ Prompt 2: Using Trigger Response, identify the trigger, emotion, and a conscious response.
