HOW PURCHASES WORK

Choose. Cart. Pay. Verify. Access.

  1. Select one or more CTJ products.
  2. Review your cart and total.
  3. Create an order intent using your email.
  4. Choose Cash App or PayPal.
  5. Pay externally using the exact order amount and order ID.
  6. Return with the transaction/reference ID.
  7. Sonly Consulting verifies the payment merchant-side.
  8. A verification email and access instructions follow after confirmation.
