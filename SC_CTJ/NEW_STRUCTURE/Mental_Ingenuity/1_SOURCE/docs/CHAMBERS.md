# Chamber Mechanic Summary

## 1. The Signal Chamber

An animated field of stone cells pulses. A true signal travels cell to cell in
one unbroken order while unscheduled noise flashes attempt to mislead. The
player watches the presentation, then reproduces the sequence on the grid.

- Pattern families: cardinal steps, diagonal weave, leaping knight rhythm
- Distractors: noise cells flash out of order between pulses
- Seeded variation: the walk is generated from a seed of chamber, level, and attempt
- Progressive complexity: grid 5, 6, then 7 with longer paths and more noise
- Controls: pointer, touch, keyboard. Replay presentation. Hints reveal the next cell
- Feedback: gold rings on locked cells, restrained shake on misreads, live region text

## 2. The Constraint Forge

An emitter fires a beam across a plate. The player rotates mirrors to seat the
beam into the core. Every rotation spends one move from a strict budget.

- Movable components: mirrors with two faces. Locked plating blocks lanes
- Undo and reset restore orientations. Moves are not refunded by undo
- Efficiency scoring compares spent moves to the authored par
- More than one valid route where the layout allows it

## 3. The Perspective Engine

Three concentric rings carry gold marks and silver decoys. The goal is to bring
every gold mark onto the sight axis at the top. Rings are gear coupled in the
later challenges, so turning one ring moves another against it.

- Mental rotation and planning across coupled actions. Par computed by BFS
- Controls: rotate buttons, keyboard Enter and Space, pointer drag on a ring
- Alignment feedback: the axis beam grows as rings lock, and aligned rings glow
- Reduced motion: rotations apply instantly with no animated sweep

## 4. The Causal Labyrinth

Three regulators feed a small integer system. Nodes update when the player
advances time. Delayed nodes remember the previous tick and memory nodes count
repeated pressure, so order and steadiness matter as much as values.

- Visible downstream consequences through node readouts and goal chips
- Delayed effects announced explicitly ("D will receive A plus B next tick")
- Resettable experiments without penalty beyond attempts
- The post level record explains the successful causal chain

## 5. The Paradox Vault

An attractive claim and a rival claim sit above an evidence board. The player
selects cards and places each into its true role: hidden assumption,
contradicting evidence, or misleading comparison. Then a verdict decides which
claim survives. An explanation panel shows why the rejected conclusion failed.

- Interactive evidence board with selectable cards and role slots
- Plausible false conclusions authored for every case
- Misplacements count as errors with gentle corrective text, never dead ends

## 6. The Summit

Three disciplines converge: a signal trace, a beam route, and a ring
alignment. Then three gates surface under a written law. The law revises once
mid climb with a visible pulse, an audio cue, and a log entry, and the old
rule becomes a trap. The player must read the revision, test both conditions,
and choose the gate.

- Integrated systems: trace, route, alignment, and a synthesis decision
- Discoverable, logical, fair rule change stated on the law panel
- Adaptive difficulty: strong prior scores raise path length and gate decoys
- No arbitrary punishment: wrong gates explain and invite another reading
