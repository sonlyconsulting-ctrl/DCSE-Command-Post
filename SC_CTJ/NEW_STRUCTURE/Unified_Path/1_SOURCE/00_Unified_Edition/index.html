WEEK 1: FOUNDATIONS OF LOGIC
Day 1 | What Is Logic?
Theme Overview: Logic is the structured framework that separates fact from opinion and belief from proof, improving decision-making in daily life.
IF-THEN Statements: Break situations into cause-and-effect relationships.
Logic isn't about being right; it's about being consistent and clear. Example: "IF I wake early, THEN I'm more productive."
Visual Aid: Describe a real-life situation where you could apply an IF-THEN flowchart. This could be a personal decision, a simple work process, or even how you troubleshoot a common issue. Outline the 'IF (condition)' and 'THEN (outcome)' steps in plain language. Reflect on how breaking it down like this helps clarify the situation.
