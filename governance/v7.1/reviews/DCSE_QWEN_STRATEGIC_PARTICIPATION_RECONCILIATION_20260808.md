## 7. Qwen Quality Improvement Standard

Every Qwen image or video workflow should use this quality ladder:

1. Lock purpose, audience, placement, aspect ratio, duration, CTA, and brand lane.
2. Supply an SC-only brand pack containing approved logo, palette, typography, voice, and negative controls.
3. Use high-quality visual references and a shot or frame specification.
4. Generate at least three materially different image variants or two video variants for high-value assets.
5. Use continuity controls for recurring people, wardrobe, products, setting, lighting, camera language, and text.
6. Reject generated typography where exact text matters. Add final text during design or editing.
7. Apply artifact, anatomy, motion, lip-sync, clipping, flicker, logo, and spelling checks.
8. Composite, upscale, color-correct, caption, mix audio, and encode outside the generator when needed.
9. Compare Qwen against one alternate surface only when value justifies the additional cost.
10. Retain prompt, model or displayed surface, source assets, outputs, selected variant, revisions, cost or credits, rights posture, and final disposition.
