- It describes the local Hub as working authority and hardcodes stale branch and repository assumptions.
- Litigation routing depends on a short keyword list that includes generic terms and may produce false routing or unnecessary content exposure.
- Device security is frozen to named computers, operating systems, and paths inside doctrine.

- A missing D06 triggers global halt rather than affected-action isolation.
