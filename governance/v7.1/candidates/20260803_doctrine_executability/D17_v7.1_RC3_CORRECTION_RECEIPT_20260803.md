## DCS-approved design decisions

1. DART means Define, Assess, Resolve, Test.
2. D17 is the universal assurance and resolution methodology.
3. D17 contains no protected-domain operating instructions.
4. DART evaluates complete work, including security, privacy, accessibility, rollback, recovery, cost, deployment, and status accuracy.
5. Correctable issues are resolved before escalation when authority and safety permit.
6. Acceptance uses declared criteria and evidence, not subjective audience difficulty.
7. Final dispositions are PASS, PASS_WITH_CORRECTIONS, FAIL, and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
8. Confidence is limited to specific inferences with an evidence basis.
9. Skills and domain workflows activate D17 through verified metadata.
10. D21 records and governs deterministic triggers.
11. D16 and D17 retain separate output and authority boundaries.
12. Orchestration is established as an early operating spine and reconciled after all doctrines are corrected.
