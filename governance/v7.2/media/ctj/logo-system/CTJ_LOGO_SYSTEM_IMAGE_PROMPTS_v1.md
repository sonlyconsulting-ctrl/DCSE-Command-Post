## 1. Visual decision

The supplied parent and first-product images are source artwork and visual references, not final logos. Their detailed scenery, maze walls, clouds, lighting, and long text do not scale reliably as a functional logo.

The logo system shall extract the central CTJ identity:

- an abstract, subliminal cross-signature revealed through geometry, negative space, light, and converging paths;
- a circular or elliptical orbit suggesting consciousness, reflection, time, and interconnected thought;
- three to five converging pathways suggesting decisions becoming clarity;
- a premium navy, silver, muted gold, and white palette;
- mature, rugged, intelligent, reflective visual language.

The symbol must not look like a literal Christian cross, crucifix, monument, pole, tower, statue, or object rising from the ground. It must not depict Jesus Christ or any human figure. The cross-like meaning should be discovered gradually within the composition rather than announced as a physical object.

## 3. Master creative prompt

Use the supplied CTJ parent artwork and first-product artwork only as visual inspiration. Do not copy the full scene. Create an advanced, premium logo-system image for Sonly Consulting’s Critical Thinker’s Journey product family.

Begin with a clean, uncluttered canvas. Reduce the source artwork to a distinctive emblem made from an abstract cross-like visual illusion, one elegant orbital ring, and three to five simplified converging paths.

The central form must not be a literal cross. Do not draw an obvious church cross, crucifix, monument, statue, pole, tower, or physical object. Do not show anything rising from the ground. Do not allow a vertical beam to grow out of a road, maze, mountain, foreground, or landscape.

Instead, create a mysterious cross-like meaning suspended in the central visual field, near the horizon or slightly above it. It should be perceived through negative space, light alignment, orbital geometry, atmospheric bands, and the convergence of pathways. The viewer should initially see an intelligent geometric and cinematic convergence. Only after looking longer should the spiritual memory of the rugged cross become apparent.

The vertical impression must be narrow, elongated, and weightless. The horizontal impression must be wider and structurally substantial, but shorter than the vertical impression. Neither element should appear physically supported. The symbol should feel revealed, suspended, and integrated into thought rather than constructed or planted in the ground.

The meaning is consciousness and subconsciousness converging through life decisions into clarity, purpose, and free thinking. Communicate reflection becoming insight, complexity becoming direction, and many paths becoming a deliberate path.

Use:

- dark navy and midnight blue;
- aged silver and restrained warm gold;
- clean white highlights;
- subtle stone and brushed-metal references only where they strengthen the emblem;
- disciplined negative space;
- premium editorial realism;
- mature cinematic atmosphere;
- no excessive glow, rays, stars, sparkle, or decorative noise.

Create a centered emblem with a strong outer silhouette that remains recognizable in black and white. The logo must work without glow, texture, shadow, or gradient. Keep the mark suitable for Stripe product imagery, SC.com product cards, app icons, favicons, social profiles, printed materials, and embroidery.

For the parent wordmark, wrap the exact title in a restrained arc above or around the emblem:

`THE CRITICAL THINKER’S JOURNEY`

For the first product lockup, use the same parent identity and add the exact product text beneath the emblem:

`THE UNIFIED PATH`

`CONSCIOUSNESS, CLARITY, AND FREE THINKING`

Use refined, highly legible typography. Do not add any other words, slogans, prices, badges, buttons, logos, watermarks, or invented text. Preserve generous spacing between the arc title, emblem, product name, and subtitle.

## 9. Technical generation and finishing guidance

### Concept generation

- Generate several symbol-only concepts before adding text.
- Select the strongest abstract cross-signature, orbit, and converging-path structure.
- Use a fixed seed for parent and child-family consistency when the tool supports it.
- Use image-to-image reference strength approximately 0.30–0.50 when supported.
- Favor structure preservation and prompt adherence over decorative detail.

### Typography

Image generators may distort exact lettering. Treat generated text as a layout reference unless it passes manual inspection. Typeset the final arc title, product name, and subtitle in Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape, or Canva.

### Required deliverables

- SVG master symbol
- transparent PNG symbol
- black symbol
- white symbol
- navy/gold/silver symbol
- parent wordmark
- first product lockup
- favicon/icon version
- Stripe-ready raster version under 2 MB

### Acceptance tests

Test each mark at 24 px, 48 px, 128 px, and 512 px. The mark passes only if:

- the abstract cross-like meaning remains visible without looking literal;
- nothing appears to rise from the ground;
- the symbol works without glow or color;
- the title is correctly spelled and readable;
- no unauthorized text or symbols appear;
- the parent and first product look like members of the same family;
- the image remains commercially appropriate for Stripe and SC.com.

## 10. Production principle
