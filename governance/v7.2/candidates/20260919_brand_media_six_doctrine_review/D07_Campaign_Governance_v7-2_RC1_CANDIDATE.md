## 1. Scope of Campaign Governance

Campaign Governance applies exclusively to market-facing positioning, lead generation, and communications.
- **Channels**: Websites, landing pages, email marketing sequences, social media posts, video scripts, and SEO/GEO/AEO assets.
- **Firewall**: Campaign files must remain free of internal architectural documentation, database schemas, and private litigation facts.

---

## 2. SEO, GEO, and AEO Standards

All search optimization assets (Search Engine Optimization, Generative Engine Optimization, and Answer Engine Optimization) must follow strict controlled guidelines:
- **No Em Dashes**: Prohibited in all web metadata, headers, page descriptions, and public paragraphs.
- **Traceability**: All public capability statements must map directly to verified corporate milestones.
- **Tone**: Maintain approachable authority, avoiding generic buzzwords or exaggerated performance claims.

## 3. v7.2 campaign and identity-media integration

### 3.1 Product, offer and factual-claim integrity
A public campaign SHALL resolve actual entity, authorized brand/product name, audience, channel, exact offer/pricing, verified payment/access paths, creative assets and source approval before publication. User research, DDNA and internal discussion may inform a candidate, but do not become customer-facing evidence or a promise by existence. No fabricated customer quotes, “verified customer” labels, “instant access”, “lifetime access”, payment badges or capability/compliance claims without specific source and verified actual runtime. Internal DDNA, governance labels, private links, PS-origin facts, secret tokens, privileged architecture and candidate statuses do not appear in public media. Separate internal production metadata from public release metadata.

### 3.2 Identity motion and public release
A release of a campaign containing a primary brand logo SHALL use its approved identity role and only authorized derivatives; it SHALL NOT present a new 6–8-second brand-motion ident as already produced because a storyboard or prompt exists. D09 holds brand hierarchy/logo and controlled terms, D12 governs media and exact approved end-plate identity, D18 governs video execution, D19 source-faithful images, D11 accessible web embedding. The 6–8s companion ident for a new approved brand package is a scoped production deliverable or DCS-approved exception; absence from an older package becomes an owned backlog, not automatic invalidation of otherwise authorized prior media. All new public video use remains DCS Level 0-gated after rights, classification, source binary, caption/audio when applicable, no-strobe and deployment QA.

### 3.3 Naming and source contradictions
`GET YOUR THINK ON!™` is listed historically as a public controlled mark in D09 while D12 inherited a blanket public suppression rule. Determine exact current owner/mark/channel authorization with DCS; do not silently market or suppress the term from one historical clause. D07 inheritance also contains obsolete entity expansions in linked doctrines: resolve SS=Smoove Spots and TI=The Initiative/training by the operative manifest and explicit current task scope; no protected PS-origin material is authorized for release. CTJ “Unified” may be positioned to an executive audience; “Executive Suite” is **not** its formally approved product name.

The inherited “no em/en dashes in public metadata” is a controlled **DCSE campaign house-style rule** at the exact scope stated in the operative approved style profile; do not pretend it is an SEO industry fact or a legal requirement. The uploaded D07 prohibits em dashes in metadata, headers, page descriptions and public paragraphs; whether to include en dashes uniformly or exceptions for quoted source text is a DCS brand-style clarification, not a basis for changing protected literal titles. Structured campaign copy should not rewrite the identity wordmark inside an approved image.

### 3.4 Distribution and attribution tests
Before a campaign goes live, validate final logo version, legible text, consent/rights, actual linked page, mobile/video/static/reduced-motion presentation, channel-specific caption/SFX requirements, source-backed claims, metadata identity, analytics privacy, actual checkout path, and public-facing contact/terms support. Verify external links in the published runtime; a screenshot is not a functional test. DCS retains final review of any material brand or release change. Store sanitized distribution metadata separately from internal Tribunal/asset provenance.

Additional challenge tests: `D07-001` private product strategy appears in public video metadata -> BLOCK. `D07-002` user payment screenshot treated as merchant verification -> FAIL. `D07-003` storyboard existence presented as released ident -> FAIL. `D07-004` final image approved but changed wordmark appears in ad -> FAIL. `D07-005` unauthorized formal rename to Executive Suite -> FAIL. `D07-006` prospective CTJ customer quote without verified source -> FAIL. `D07-007` public campaign passes code preview only, broken live CTA -> release gate fails.
