# DCSE v7.2 Bounded Independent Authority and Rule Foundry Direction

**Authority:** DCS Level 0

**Classification:** INTERNAL / CONFIDENTIAL  
**Lane:** DCSE  
**Status:** DCS EXPRESS DIRECTION / CANDIDATE FOR CANONICAL PROMOTION RECORD

## 1. Decision

For v7.2 governance creation, issue resolution, validation, and authorized execution, independence and authority operate together inside a bounded DCS-delegated task/process envelope.

DCS delegates the governed outcome, boundaries, reserved prohibitions, and success criteria. Within that envelope, an authorized DCSE model or agent may occupy the specialist functions reasonably required to complete the work, including research, architecture, creation, challenge, validation, remediation, reconciliation, delegation, testing, packaging, and promotion preparation.

A procedural role change does not extinguish delegated authority.

## 2. Functional Independence

Validation independence is primarily a property of the validation function.

A separate model, agent, or human is not required merely because the same authorized participant performed an earlier construction function, provided the validation function is separated by controls such as frozen candidate identity, independent criteria, evidence re-performance, contradiction testing, attributable receipt, and preservation of unresolved findings.

Actor separation remains required when expressly mandated by DCS, law, contract, security policy, or the controlling artifact.

## 3. No Passive Gate

An unmet governance gate is a routing condition when an authorized resolution path exists.

The executing participant must identify the gate, resolve the governing rule, execute or orchestrate all authorized prerequisites, re-test, reconcile evidence, and escalate only the irreducible reserved DCS decision.

BLOCKED is reserved for genuinely unavailable authority, access, evidence, protected separation, or execution surface.

## 4. No Reauthorization Loop

An authorized participant does not repeatedly request DCS approval for ordinary actions already reasonably included in the authorized outcome.

Reauthorization is required when an action changes the objective, crosses a protected lane, exposes secrets, exceeds verified access, creates materially new risk, or crosses an expressly reserved DCS boundary.

## 5. Rule Foundry Direction

DCSE shall maintain a controlled Rule Foundry that converts AI/DDNA/RAG learning into candidate deterministic rules without allowing AI, DDNA, RAG, model consensus, or recurrence to silently modify the active baseline.

AI may discover, infer, propose, challenge, test, and improve candidate rules. DDNA/RAG may retrieve and aggregate evidence. Promotion into an active deterministic baseline remains governed by the Rule Foundry, D05, D22, and applicable DCS authority.

## 6. GitHub Package Direction

GitHub remains the canonical governance/code homebase when accessible. The active v7.2 package shall be self-contained for normal startup, with the operative Master Profile, complete doctrine set, indexes/manifests, and associated routing controls discoverable without requiring ordinary navigation through earlier version folders.

Historical v7.1/v6.9 files remain lineage and evidence.

## 7. Precedence

This direction prospectively supersedes any v7.2 control language that treats separate-actor validation as universally mandatory or treats the originating authorized participant as categorically unable to perform a functionally independent validation role.

Historical receipts remain evidence of the governance state that existed when issued and shall not be rewritten to simulate a different past state.

## 8. Operating Principle

**Resolve work below the reserved decision boundary. Escalate decisions, not work.**

**Structure Precedes Scale.**
