# DCSE Content and Artifact Generation Standard v1

**Document ID:** DCSE-CONTENT-ARTIFACT-GEN-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** APPROVED BY DCS-DIR-20260911-001 / ACTIVE UPON CANONICAL PR #83 MERGE  
**Parent:** DCSE v7.2 Rule Foundry  
**Applies To:** content, webpages, product pages, campaigns, images, video concepts, audio, documents, metadata, reusable creative assets.

## 1. Objective

Generate varied, high-quality work without template drift while preserving deterministic enterprise boundaries.

Creative freedom is maximized inside an explicit artifact contract.

## 2. Required Generation Packet

Before material generation, resolve:

- task ID;
- lane and entity;
- product or campaign;
- audience/persona;
- artifact type and destination;
- release posture;
- product truth/source packet;
- claim support;
- voice/tone;
- brand/design rules;
- typography roles;
- required CTA;
- channel/format requirements;
- PS/PPR and secret scans;
- applicable executable rules;
- model/tool capability needs;
- exit criteria.

## 3. Product Genome

For product work, establish at minimum:

- product name;
- product truth;
- intended user/customer;
- problem/desire;
- promise;
- differentiator;
- offer;
- proof/support;
- objections;
- visual personality;
- narrative/story;
- CTA;
- channels;
- required artifacts;
- release gates.

The six initial product tracks are: **CTJ, Vow N Go, TSL, B4L, X50, Beauty**.

Each product runs through the same governed pipeline while preserving its own genome.

## 4. Sequential-Parallel Production

Shared sequence:

`GENOME -> RETRIEVE -> DIVERGE -> CHALLENGE -> CONVERGE -> PRODUCE -> INSPECT -> PACKAGE -> RELEASE -> LEARN`

The six products may run in parallel, but no downstream artifact may outrun the minimum genome and rule packet required for that artifact.

Reusable improvements discovered in an earlier product may be adopted by later products after rule/reuse checks.

## 5. Retrieval

Retrieve only relevant:

- product history;
- validated DDNA;
- brand identity;
- audience/persona material;
- prior approved/rejected artifacts;
- campaign findings;
- reusable components and workflows.

Retrieval adds context, not authority.

## 6. Divergence

Generate materially different directions rather than minor rewrites.

As applicable, use distinct specialist functions for:

- product strategy;
- sales logic;
- audience psychology;
- story;
- copy;
- typography;
- visual direction;
- image generation;
- video concept;
- skeptical challenge.

## 7. Convergence

A judge/reconciler evaluates candidates against:

- product truth;
- audience fit;
- differentiation;
- clarity;
- brand/entity fit;
- originality;
- claim support;
- artifact/channel requirements;
- deterministic rule compliance;
- likely conversion or communication effectiveness.

Model disagreement is evidence to inspect, not a defect to suppress.

## 8. Invariants

All generated artifacts must:

- preserve declared lane/entity;
- exclude unauthorized PS/PPR material;
- exclude secrets and internal credentials;
- distinguish Verified, Likely, and Unknown where factual status matters;
- avoid unsupported capability/performance claims;
- suppress internal-only governance terminology from public content unless expressly authorized;
- preserve controlled product/entity names;
- satisfy required accessibility and destination constraints;
- identify material AI-generated factual uncertainty before release.

## 9. Anti-Slop Rules

A candidate fails quality review when it materially relies on:

- generic AI filler or unsupported superlatives;
- interchangeable section patterns unrelated to the product;
- repeated empty phrases such as transformation, innovation, seamless, revolutionary without specific evidence;
- invented testimonials, metrics, awards, clients, outcomes, or product capabilities;
- visual style that ignores the product genome;
- copy that could be swapped to another product without meaningful change.

## 10. Typography

Typography is governed as functional design intelligence.

Every substantial visual/web artifact should define:

- display role;
- body role;
- UI/label role where applicable;
- hierarchy;
- weight;
- line length;
- spacing;
- contrast;
- target device;
- audience readability;
- product personality.

A model may propose type systems. Final implementation must satisfy the applicable brand, accessibility, and destination rules.

## 11. Images

Image generation follows:

`VISUAL BRIEF -> MODEL/TOOL SELECTION -> VARIANTS -> VISUAL INSPECTION -> BRAND/ARTIFACT QA -> HUMAN/PRODUCT SELECTION`

Requirements are selected before the model. The orchestrator may dynamically choose among eligible image models based on task class, capability, cost, latency, reference-image support, fidelity, and prior DCSE performance.

## 12. Video

Video begins with story and product truth:

`RETRIEVAL -> STORY MINING -> CONCEPTS -> SCRIPT -> BEATS -> STORYBOARD -> SHOT LANGUAGE -> GENERATION -> EDIT/CRITIQUE`

Visual novelty without narrative coherence is a non-pass for story-led artifacts.

## 13. Public Content Gate

Before public release verify:

- claim support;
- entity firewall;
- no internal governance exposure;
- no PS/PPR spill;
- no secrets;
- CTA correctness;
- contact/link correctness;
- metadata;
- accessibility;
- mobile/destination behavior;
- final visual/copy coherence.

## 14. Learning Loop

After selection/publication, capture:

- winning concept;
- rejected alternatives and why;
- model/tool used;
- rule failures;
- audience/channel performance when available;
- reusable design/content patterns;
- next candidate rules.

Observed performance may create new Rule Foundry candidates but does not mutate the active ruleset.

## 15. Release States

`DRAFT -> REVIEW -> CANDIDATE -> APPROVED_FOR_STATED_USE -> PUBLISHED/DEPLOYED`

Authority-bearing promotion remains separate from ordinary product/publication lifecycle where applicable.
