**Authority:** DCS Level 0  
**Lane:** SC / DCSE Enterprise, with SS content lineage as applicable

## 1. Interview Operating Rule

The interview is not a brainstorming exercise alone. Each answer should be classified as:

- VERIFIED CURRENT STATE
- DESIRED FUTURE STATE
- CONSTRAINT
- ASSUMPTION
- OPEN DECISION
- REJECTED DIRECTION
- TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS
- RULE CANDIDATE

For each substantive answer capture:

1. answer;
2. source/evidence;
3. confidence;
4. owner;
5. decision required;
6. downstream product impact;
7. likely rule family;
8. acceptance test.
