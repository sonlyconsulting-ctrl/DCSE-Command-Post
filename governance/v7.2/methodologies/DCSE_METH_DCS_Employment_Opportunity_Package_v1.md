## 1. Scope

DCS Employment is the direct DCS income and opportunity workspace. It includes employment, contract, freelance, consulting, advisory, project services, business opportunities, recruiter engagement, professional positioning, and related income paths tied to verified DCS capability.

SC remains the enterprise operations and commercial vehicle for repeatable products, services, client delivery systems, websites, implementation, and commercial offerings. Cross-entity ventures and portfolio-level decisions route to DCS Enterprise. Governance and control architecture route to DCSE Command Post.

This methodology modernizes the legacy three-track Employment Command Center without discarding its package-development strengths.

## 2. Opportunity Classification

Classify before producing strategy or artifacts:

1. **Employment:** W-2 or equivalent direct employment.
2. **Contract:** staffing, 1099, temporary, corp-to-corp, fractional, or project contract.
3. **Freelance:** defined deliverable sold directly by DCS.
4. **Consulting / Advisory:** expertise-led analysis, architecture, governance, facilitation, implementation, or decision support.
5. **Business Opportunity:** partnership, venture, licensing, referral, reseller, collaboration, or similar income opportunity.
6. **SC Commercial Opportunity:** repeatable product/service or client delivery better owned by Sonly Consulting.
7. **Mixed:** decompose components, assign ownership, dependencies, handoffs, and revenue responsibility.

The historical Stretch/Stars, Within-Limits, and Freelance/Part-Time tracks remain useful segmentation patterns but are not exclusive classifications.

## 3. Intake and Decision Frame

Capture only facts material to the decision:

- source and counterparty;
- role/problem/opportunity;
- buyer or hiring authority;
- engagement type;
- scope and deliverables;
- location/work arrangement;
- schedule/duration;
- requirements and selection process;
- compensation, rate, pricing, or revenue model;
- payment/IP/contract terms when applicable;
- timing and next gate;
- required proof/evidence;
- competitive position;
- delivery capacity;
- repeatability and SC handoff potential;
- material risks and unknowns.

Ask only when missing information materially changes authority, economics, legality, privacy, release posture, evidence, routing, or the recommendation.

## 4. Evidence and Fit Matrix

Separate **Verified**, **Likely**, and **Unknown**. Never invent employers, clients, dates, titles, technologies, certifications, metrics, rates, revenue, responsibilities, or capabilities.

Create an evidence map for material opportunities:

| Requirement / Need | Evidence | Status | Gap | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Target requirement | Verified source or artifact | Verified/Likely/Unknown | None/Gap | Preserve/Reframe/Research/Do not claim |

DCSE work becomes professional evidence only when DCS has actually designed, built, tested, governed, operated, delivered, evaluated, or can competently explain it.

Preserve material contradictions and unresolved source conflicts. Do not smooth them away for readability.

## 5. Package Selection

Select the smallest effective package for the opportunity.

### Employment / Contract Package
May include:
- tailored resume;
- cover letter or cover email;
- recruiter response;
- LinkedIn/profile alignment;
- capability/evidence supplement;
- interview brief;
- compensation/rate brief;
- follow-up record.

### Freelance / Consulting Package
May include:
- one-page capability statement;
- scoped proposal;
- evidence/portfolio links;
- service description;
- pricing/rate structure;
- delivery assumptions;
- terms/questions;
- outreach message;
- follow-up record.

### Business Opportunity Package
May include:
- opportunity assessment;
- value/revenue model;
- role and ownership map;
- risk/dependency analysis;
- partner/counterparty questions;
- DCS vs SC routing decision;
- decision memo;
- next-gate action.

Do not force every revenue opportunity into a resume/job-search workflow.

## 6. Baseline Preservation and Precision Editing

Approved resumes, bios, proposals, capability statements, pricing structures, portfolio narratives, outreach templates, and recurring artifacts are controlled baselines.

For tailoring:

1. identify the authoritative baseline;
2. compare target needs against baseline evidence;
3. identify exact deltas;
4. preserve unaffected content, accomplishments, dates, structure, footer, formatting, and approved voice;
5. change only justified wording, ordering, emphasis, proof points, pricing, or terms;
6. prefer repositioning verified evidence over creating new claims;
7. backward-check each material change against target need and evidence;
8. run anti-slop, duplication, inflation, keyword-stuffing, audience-fit, and formatting-drift review;
9. produce a change register when changes are material.

No global rewrite merely because a model can produce a newer-sounding version.

## 7. Reasoning and Quality Control

Use forward/backward chaining, inductive/deductive analysis, alternative generation, tradeoff comparison, and adversarial review.

For material choices:
- define the objective;
- generate alternatives;
- test assumptions and economics;
- compare tradeoffs and risks;
- challenge weak claims and failure modes;
- reverse-check the target outcome;
- correct contradictions, omissions, unsupported claims, and generic AI output;
- advance the strongest supported result.

The first plausible strategy, title, positioning statement, package, rate, or narrative is a candidate, not final.

## 8. Voice and Representation

DCS opportunity voice is direct, measured, senior, selective, evidence-based, commercially aware, and natural.

Employment output must be hiring/recruiter appropriate. Consulting/freelance output emphasizes problem, value, evidence, scope, outcomes, and delivery confidence. Business-opportunity analysis is executive, economic, and risk-aware. SC commercial material follows SC brand/voice controls.

Do not invent “our team,” “our company,” or a workforce when representing DCS as a solo operator. Avoid generic business language, shallow AI hype, desperation, unsupported superlatives, and empty motivation.

## 9. Submission / Release Gate

Before external delivery verify:

- correct opportunity classification and owner;
- current source and counterparty facts;
- evidence support for every material claim;
- correct baseline/version;
- package completeness;
- audience and destination fit;
- contact details and links;
- formatting/render integrity;
- no internal doctrine, hidden routing, Tribunal records, credentials, private notes, or protected source spill;
- accessibility claims use “designed to target accessibility standards” unless formal audit supports stronger language;
- required approval recorded.

A draft or render does not become approved merely because it exists.

## 10. Engagement Tracking Architecture

Every material revenue pursuit receives an engagement record when it becomes more than a passing lead. The tracking workflow is architectural and may be implemented in Supabase, the Command Post dashboard, a governed spreadsheet, CRM, or another approved system without changing this lifecycle.

### 10.1 Engagement Identity

Use a durable identifier:

`DCS-ENG-[YYYYMMDD]-[###]`

Preserve the same engagement ID across resumes, proposals, emails, interviews, negotiations, handoffs, delivery records, follow-ups, and closeout evidence.

### 10.2 Lifecycle

Normal forward path:

`CAPTURED -> QUALIFIED -> PURSUIT_DECISION -> PACKAGE_PREP -> SUBMITTED_OR_PROPOSED -> ACTIVE_CONVERSATION -> INTERVIEW_OR_DISCOVERY -> NEGOTIATION -> WON_OR_ENGAGED -> ACTIVE_DELIVERY -> COMPLETED -> FOLLOW_UP`

Alternate terminal or routing states:

- `DECLINED`
- `LOST`
- `WITHDRAWN`
- `DORMANT`
- `ROUTED_TO_SC`
- `ROUTED_TO_DCS_ENTERPRISE`
- `BLOCKED`

A stage change must be supported by an observable event, not optimism or model inference.

### 10.3 Required Engagement Record

Track, when applicable:

- engagement_id;
- opportunity classification;
- source/channel;
- counterparty/company/client;
- primary contact and role;
- DCS/SC ownership;
- current stage;
- opened date;
- last activity date;
- next action;
- next-action owner;
- next-action due date;
- target role/service/opportunity;
- package/version used;
- compensation/rate/pricing/revenue model;
- estimated value and confidence basis;
- probability or confidence band, if used;
- interviews/discovery calls/meetings;
- proposal/submission dates;
- negotiation position;
- blockers and unknowns;
- evidence links;
- related Task IDs;
- outcome;
- outcome reason;
- realized revenue/compensation when known;
- follow-up date;
- lessons learned.

Never record a speculative value as realized revenue.

### 10.4 Stage Transition Rules

- **CAPTURED -> QUALIFIED:** minimum identity, source, opportunity type, and plausible fit established.
- **QUALIFIED -> PURSUIT_DECISION:** fit, economics, risk, and ownership reviewed.
- **PURSUIT_DECISION -> PACKAGE_PREP:** DCS elects to pursue or the task clearly authorizes pursuit.
- **PACKAGE_PREP -> SUBMITTED_OR_PROPOSED:** external submission/proposal is actually sent.
- **SUBMITTED_OR_PROPOSED -> ACTIVE_CONVERSATION:** counterparty responds substantively.
- **ACTIVE_CONVERSATION -> INTERVIEW_OR_DISCOVERY:** scheduled or completed evaluative/discovery interaction.
- **INTERVIEW_OR_DISCOVERY -> NEGOTIATION:** compensation, rate, scope, terms, or offer becomes an active decision.
- **NEGOTIATION -> WON_OR_ENGAGED:** offer/engagement is accepted or contract/authorization is established.
- **WON_OR_ENGAGED -> ACTIVE_DELIVERY:** performance/delivery actually begins.
- **ACTIVE_DELIVERY -> COMPLETED:** agreed work or employment engagement phase closes and required obligations are satisfied.
- **COMPLETED -> FOLLOW_UP:** relationship maintenance, referral, renewal, testimonial, next opportunity, or retrospective.

### 10.5 Pipeline Review

At weekly or task-relevant review, surface:

- active engagements by stage;
- next actions due;
- stale engagements with no activity;
- value/revenue by confidence state;
- conversion by opportunity class and source;
- package versions used;
- unresolved blockers;
- SC handoff candidates;
- wins, losses, withdrawals, and outcome reasons.

Historical benchmark percentages are not controlling. Use observed DCS pipeline data and current market evidence.

### 10.6 Engagement Closeout

Closeout must record:
- final state;
- decision/outcome date;
- outcome reason;
- actual compensation/revenue when known;
- artifacts used;
- material feedback;
- reusable evidence created;
- follow-up obligation;
- lessons for future targeting/package/pricing.

Substantive engagement closeout must satisfy the v7.2 Completion Evidence Collector when governed artifacts, system writes, deployments, or material decisions are involved.

## 11. Tracking and Learning

Use engagement records inductively to improve:
- package choice;
- positioning;
- target selection;
- pricing/rates;
- platform/channel effectiveness;
- evidence gaps;
- timing/follow-up;
- reusable templates;
- SC routing;
- opportunity qualification.

## 12. Outputs

Authorized outputs include Opportunity Assessment, Engagement Record, Pipeline Review, Fit Matrix, Resume Delta Plan, Resume/Letter Package, Recruiter Response, Interview Brief, Negotiation Brief, Freelance Scope, Consulting Proposal, Capability Statement, Pricing Analysis, Business/Venture Decision Memo, Skill Evidence Record, Handoff Packet, Change Register, and Closeout Report.

## 13. Exit Criteria

Complete only when:
- opportunity is correctly classified;
- engagement is tracked when material;
- ownership/routing is clear;
- claims are evidence-grounded;
- economics and risks are addressed where relevant;
- package is audience-fit and internally consistent;
- controlled baselines are preserved;
- obvious AI slop and unsupported claims are removed;
- release controls are satisfied;
- next action and owner are clear;
- final stage and outcome are recorded when the pursuit is closed.

**Structure Precedes Scale.**
