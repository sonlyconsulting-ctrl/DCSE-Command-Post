## 1. Executive Doctrine: Structure Precedes Scale in Media

Within the DCSE estate, video generation and storytelling are governed assets, not open-ended creative experiments. Generating raw video prompts without strict narrative and technical constraints results in model drift, generic aesthetics, brand dilution, and wasted GPU compute.

All video production is categorized into three structured duration frameworks and executed through the **"From Me, For Me" (FMFM)** extraction protocol.

### Framework 1: The Promo / Social Short ($\le 60$ Seconds)
- **Primary Use:** Product teasers, quick story hooks, social awareness (Shorts, Reels, TikTok).
- **Narrative Architecture:** AI-Optimized AIDA Formula (proven 93% retention structure):
  - **0–5 Seconds (The Hook):** Visual arrest, controversial proposition, or direct confrontation of the viewer's friction.
  - **5–45 Seconds (The Value Bomb):** Dense informational core. Demonstrates the product in motion or provides immediate insight without rambling.
  - **45–60 Seconds (The CTA):** Behavior-predicted call to action (e.g., "Begin the Strategic Clarity Assessment").
- **Production Profiles:**
  - *Lean/Social:* Rapid cuts, bold kinetic captions, trending ambient bed.
  - *Cinematic:* Generated via Sora or Veo 2; FPV tracking camera moves; high-fidelity physics.

### Framework 2: The Medium-Form Explainer / Product Review (1 to 3 Minutes)
- **Primary Use:** SC.com / SS.com website hero embeds, LinkedIn executive briefings, detailed product breakdowns, case studies.
- **Narrative Architecture:** The 1-2-3 Grisham Blueprint (legal thriller intensity and pacing):
  - **The Prompt (0–30 Seconds):** State the problem with a "bang." Establish immediate operational friction or decision paralysis.
  - **The Process (30s–2 Minutes):** Deconstruct the friction into manageable structural components. Demonstrate the solution using right-branching (Subject-Verb-Object) sentences for forward propulsion.
  - **The Resolve (2–3 Minutes):** Definitive conclusion and single unshakeable next action.
- **Production Profiles:**
  - *Standard:* Precision image sequencing, structured motion typography, clean voiceover.
  - *High-End:* ARRI Alexa 65 aesthetic, 35mm prime lens characteristics, natural daylight illumination, professional ElevenLabs voiceover.

### Framework 3: The Long-Form Strategic Story (3 to 5 Minutes)
- **Primary Use:** Founder narratives, brand documentaries, CTJ curriculum overviews, enterprise keynote showcases.
- **Narrative Architecture:** The Thought Cycle™ Arc (mapped to DCSE cognitive discipline):
  - **Pre-Game (0–1 Min):** The Neutral Beginning. Establish the status quo and unexamined assumptions.
  - **Tip-Off & Halftime (1–3 Mins):** Execution under real-world constraints. Encountering chaos, shadow work, contradiction mapping, and structured audit pauses.
  - **Final Buzzer & Post-Game (3–4 Mins):** The scoreboard as data. Extraction of meaning, quantifiable ROI, and personal transformation.
  - **What's Next (4–5 Mins):** Deliberate re-entry. Closing the loop with a permanent structural commitment.
- **Production Profiles:**
  - *Mid-Tier:* Smooth voiceovers, structured slides, curated archival B-roll.
  - *Cinematic Prestige:* 4K DCI resolution at 24fps, moody warm DCI-P3 color grading, minimal orchestral score, resonant voice archetype (Morgan Freeman / Michael B. Jordan resonance).

## 3. The "From Me, For Me" (FMFM) Socratic Extraction Protocol

Generic prompt crafting produces generic AI slop. The FMFM Protocol transforms the AI agent into an executive cinematographer, technical director, and gap analyst.

### The 7 Pillars (Aligned with V7.3-4 Doctrine)
1. **Pillar 1: The Neutral Beginning (Current State / Friction):** What is the baseline operational reality or problem being addressed?
2. **Pillar 2: Subject & Kinetic Action:** What is the exact noun and active verb? (Specific items over generic categories).
3. **Pillar 3: Environment & Setting:** Time of day, atmospheric conditions, architectural context.
4. **Pillar 4: Camera Mechanics & Lens:** Specific cinematographic camera movement (single movement per shot to avoid diffusion warping) and lens profile (e.g., 35mm prime, anamorphic).
5. **Pillar 5: Lighting Design:** Light source, direction, quality, color temperature, and shadow falloff.
6. **Pillar 6: Audio & Narration:** Voice archetype, words-per-minute (WPM) cadence, ambient sound bed, and audio ducking rules.
7. **Pillar 7: The "What's Next" Constraint (The Business Engine):** The definitive commercial destination (CaaS retainer, CTJ purchase, assessment completion, discovery call).

### Phase 2: AI Gap Analysis & Computational Alignment
Before any video kernel is generated, the AI performs automated auditing:
- **Conflict Detection:** Identifies contradictory cinematographic constraints (e.g., wide aerial drone shot combined with shallow macro bokeh).
- **Platform Viability Check:** Determines whether the prompt is optimized for Luma Dream Machine (camera maneuvers), Sora / Veo 2 (photorealism and physics), or Runway Gen-3 Alpha (stylized transitions).
- **Asset Inventory Tracking:** Cross-references DCSE vaults and Wix libraries to distinguish reusable existing B-roll from required new renders.

## 4. Evolution of Intake Diagnostic: From Jotform to Multimodal Interactive Video Diagnostic

### 4.1 Root Cause of Static Form Friction
The historical "Sonly Consulting Strategic Intake and Reconnaissance" Jotform (tested with Ivan C) utilized the AI agent Chandra. While thorough, the user experience stalled because:
- A static multi-page web form feels like unpaid administrative labor.
- It lacks dynamic visual gratification and conversational feedback.
- It fails to demonstrate the "Arbitrage on Intelligence" that SC promises.

### 4.2 The Multimodal "Home Builder" Diagnostic Specification
The new diagnostic re-engineers intake into an interactive design studio resembling a luxury home customizer:
- **Multimodal Intake:**
  - *Audio Bridge:* Web Audio / MediaRecorder capturing conversational spoken input.
  - *Asset Drag-and-Drop:* Users upload reference images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and ZIP archives directly into the canvas.
- **Curated Visual Canvas:**
  - Instead of open chat text, the interface dynamically renders curated A/B visual choices, palette swatches, and layout blueprints as the conversation progresses.
- **Continuous Gap Analysis & Contradiction Detection:**
  - If a user verbally requests "ultra-minimalist monochrome" but uploads a heavily saturated, multi-color mood board, the engine flags the friction immediately.
  - The AI pauses, highlights the contradiction visually, and offers 3 curated resolution paths before advancing to the next pillar.
- **The Level 0 Authority Gate:**
  - The diagnostic compiles the complete 7-Pillar specification into a final Blueprint Manifest. No rendering or deployment occurs until the user executes an explicit sign-off.

### 4.3 Staging Strategy: Disposable Sandbox to Sovereign Infrastructure

```
┌───────────────────────────────────┐       ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
│     PHASE 1: DISPOSABLE SANDBOX   │       │    PHASE 2: SOVEREIGN PRODUCTION  │
│        (Wix Free Plan Sandbox)    │ ────> │   (Vercel / Netlify + Supabase)   │
├───────────────────────────────────┤       ├───────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Zero-cost rapid prototyping     │       │ • Custom domain (sonlyconsulting) │
│ • Native Wix CMS state ledger     │       │ • Supabase PostgreSQL ledger      │
│ • Velo backend (.jsw) pure JS     │       │ • Vercel Serverless Edge API      │
│ • Internal logic validation only  │       │ • React / Next.js Custom UI       │
└───────────────────────────────────┘       └───────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Phase 1 (Wix Sandbox):** Validates the Socratic questioning tree, file parsing, and contradiction detection logic at zero infrastructure cost. Business logic is strictly isolated in pure JavaScript `.jsw` backend modules (JSON input $\rightarrow$ JSON output).
- **Phase 2 (Sovereign Cutover):** The `.jsw` modules port verbatim to Vercel Serverless Edge Functions, the CMS schema is mapped to Supabase PostgreSQL, and the frontend is rendered via Next.js on the production custom domain.
