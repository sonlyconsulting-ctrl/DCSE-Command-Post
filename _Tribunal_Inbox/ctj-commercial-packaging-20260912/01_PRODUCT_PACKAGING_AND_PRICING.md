Pricing and packaging are treated as one architecture.

The price should tell the same story as the product ladder:

Membership is a parallel access/value path. It does not replace a la carte Keeper purchases.

## Membership relationship

Do not redesign existing SC membership plans in order to launch CTJ.

Candidate rule:
- a la carte purchase = product ownership/Keeper treatment as approved
- membership = ongoing access plus member-only continuing value
- the two can coexist
- an owner should not be penalized for also becoming a member
- a member should not lose already vested/direct-purchase Keeper products on cancellation

Focus & Flow is especially suitable for member-only expansion beyond the base edition:
- rotating prompt packs
- longitudinal history
- new session modes
- cross-device history
- member-specific insights

Mental Ingenuity can similarly receive future member-only challenge expansions without changing the owned base game.
