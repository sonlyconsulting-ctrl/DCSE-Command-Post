## Market observation

The mainstream premium individual AI subscription price clusters tightly around $20/month.

That creates a useful customer reference point, but it does not mean CTJ should be priced as model access.

CTJ differences:
- structured product experience
- deterministic curriculum/mechanics
- owned a la carte products where approved
- portable artifacts/results
- no requirement to maintain an AI subscription to keep the owned base experience
- current product-core runtime is local-first and does not depend on paid external AI APIs
