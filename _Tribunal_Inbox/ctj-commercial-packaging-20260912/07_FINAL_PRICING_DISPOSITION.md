## Future open-source AI principle

DCS intends to develop/use an open-source model path for future AI capability.

Commercial rule:
Do not reduce base product prices merely because local/open-source inference can lower marginal model cost.

Price the owned CTJ products on customer value and product scope.

Keep future variable-cost AI capability separate from the owned base product where appropriate:
- member-only advanced AI
- optional AI enhancement
- usage-governed AI tier
- local/open-source inference when economically and technically appropriate

Owned base products should continue to function without requiring a paid frontier-model subscription unless DCS explicitly changes that architecture.

## Repricing triggers

Do not repeatedly reprice based on minor competitor changes.

Reopen pricing only if one of the following becomes material:
1. actual production unit economics materially weaken margin
2. payment/support/hosting/AI variable cost materially changes
3. product scope materially expands or contracts
4. CTJ market positioning materially changes
5. mainstream premium-AI reference pricing shifts enough to change customer expectations
6. DCS intentionally changes membership/Keeper economics
