## Core progression

### Discover
Strategic Clarity Assessment
- locates current patterns
- produces Strategic Clarity Profile
- routes attention toward Parts 1, 2, and 3
- does not diagnose or rank the person

### Learn 1
Part 1: Building Clarity and Confidence
- claims, evidence, assumptions, premises
- deductive and inductive reasoning
- bias and fallacy recognition
- outcome: clearer reasoning discipline

### Learn 2
Part 2: Exploring Ideas and Making Moves
- argument testing
- evidence in action
- causation
- experimentation
- failure, adaptation, creative movement
- outcome: evidence-guided action

### Learn 3
Part 3: Finding Meaning and Balance
- mindset
- emotion
- cognitive scripts
- decision clarity
- purpose, values, triggers, intentional living
- outcome: aligned judgment and balance

### Practice
CTJ: Focus & Flow
- short reset
- bias priming
- focused journal prompt
- Thinking Partner
- one concrete next action
- role: recurring companion, not a prerequisite

### Prove
Mental Ingenuity
- observable reasoning behavior under constraints
- signal, constraint, perspective, causal systems, contradiction, adaptation
- Reasoning Records and Reasoning Signature
- role: interactive proving ground, not diagnosis and not a replacement for Unified

### Integrate
Unified Edition: The Complete Logic Map
- complex situations
- fact/assumption separation
- alternatives
- values and consequences
- decision
- revision conditions
- final live decision synthesis
- role: capstone, not a replay bundle

## Candidate journey architecture

Core required learning path:
SCA -> Part 1 -> Part 2 -> Part 3 -> Unified

Sidecar:
Focus & Flow may be used throughout the journey and is not a progression gate.

Optional proving ground:
Mental Ingenuity fits after sufficient foundation. Candidate placement is after Part 2 or after Part 3. Exact entitlement/unlock timing remains a DCS master-review decision.

## Backward check

Unified requires integration of clarity, action, values, emotion, and revision.
Those inputs are developed across Parts 1 through 3.
SCA locates starting patterns.
Focus & Flow maintains practice.
Mental Ingenuity provides behavioral evidence under constraint.

No product is required to impersonate another product to make the progression work.
