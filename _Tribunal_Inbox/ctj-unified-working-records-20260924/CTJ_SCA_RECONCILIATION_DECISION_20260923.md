## Content decision

The target remains 31 scored questions, but `31` is not permission to preserve weak or duplicated items. The question bank must be validated as 31 distinct, comprehensible measures.

For each question record:

- stable question ID;
- CTJ pillar and construct;
- exact prompt;
- response-scale type;
- scoring direction;
- weight, defaulting to 1.0;
- result rule affected;
- accessibility label;
- content version and approval state.

The current variants must be reviewed before promotion:

- `cv2`, `cv2a`, and `cv2b`;
- `cv4`, `cv4a`, and `cv4b`;
- `sr7`, `sr7a`, `sr7b`, and `sr7c`;
- `at5`, `at5a`, `at5b`, and `at5c`.

The review may retain more than one item only when each measures a distinct construct and its influence on the result is intentional.

## Static and AI boundary

### Deterministic core

The following work without AI:

- all 31 scored questions;
- response validation;
- scoring and tie resolution;
- section checkpoints;
- Strategic Clarity Profile;
- Pattern Analysis;
- Immediate Blueprint;
- seven-day action order when approved;
- local save, resume, reset, export, and print;
- recommended CTJ entry point based on governed rules.

### Optional AI companion

AI may be offered after deterministic results to:

- explain the profile in plain language;
- compare Then, Now, and Next implications;
- explore a user-provided scenario through the result pattern;
- generate counterviews and questions;
- help convert the blueprint into options;
- connect an authorized result snapshot to Unified CTJ Exchange.

AI may not:

- change question wording during a scored assessment;
- decide or alter scores, classifications, or product recommendations;
- infer protected traits or mental-health conditions;
- claim psychometric validation;
- create facts about the user;
- transmit responses without informed consent;
- block delivery of the complete static result.

This preserves comparability, cost control, privacy, offline resilience, and credibility while still giving CTJ a modern adaptive layer.

## Validation approach

Testing all `5^31` response combinations individually is unnecessary and infeasible. Validate the rules with:

1. question and construct traceability;
2. all-minimum, all-midpoint, and all-maximum fixtures;
3. each-pillar-dominant fixtures;
4. every tie and near-tie boundary;
5. every classification threshold plus values immediately above and below it;
6. every reverse-scored item;
7. missing-answer, resume, reset, and corrupted-state cases;
8. property tests ensuring scores remain within defined bounds;
9. deterministic replay from stored answers;
10. AI-off, AI-timeout, and AI-invalid-output cases;
11. mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, and 200 percent zoom checks;
12. offline or dependency-failure behavior when local operation is claimed.
