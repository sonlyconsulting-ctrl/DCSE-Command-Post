## 6. Mandatory Out-of-Town Economics Gate

Recurring out-of-town onsite opportunities require consistent pre-screening that includes at minimum:

- cost of living and housing differential;
- airfare or mileage;
- lodging;
- local transportation, parking, and tolls;
- meals/per diem;
- onsite cadence and nights away;
- reimbursement or relocation support;
- W2/C2C/1099 and benefits structure;
- tax differences where relevant;
- nearby lower-cost communities;
- base professional rate;
- all-inclusive break-even rate;
- effective rate after unreimbursed costs;
- alternative onsite cadence scenarios.

Required decision output: `GO`, `CONDITIONAL GO`, or `NO-GO` with assumptions visible.
