Every file, export, artifact, conversation, document, HTML asset, spreadsheet, PDF, image, code file, and database record must be excavated, fingerprinted, classified, and assigned governance requirements before chunking, embedding, promotion, or retrieval use is permitted.

### Rule 002: Lane Isolation

PS, SC, SS, TI, DCS, DDNA, and Shared content must remain isolated unless explicitly promoted through governance. PS content shall not be inserted into SC/SS repositories or public-facing retrieval paths without authorized PS governance.

### Rule 003: Single Authority Reference Model

Canonical governance directives reside in DCSE-DDNA. Other repositories may store references, acknowledgements, or operational pointers, but shall not become duplicate sources of authority unless DCS expressly promotes them.
