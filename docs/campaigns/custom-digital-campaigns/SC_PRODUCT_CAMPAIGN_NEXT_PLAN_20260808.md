## 1. DCS Direction Recorded

The SC website must do more than describe capabilities. It must show the work.

The design will use:

- a recognizable, authored editorial shell rather than a generic agency template;
- the DCSE Dossier as the central public proof and storytelling mechanism;
- clean, restrained product and case-study pages;
- business-facing service architecture where it improves clarity;
- distinct SC and SS fingerprints built on related DDNA;
- a protected PS architecture that never exposes or cross-indexes PS content.

Reference roles:

| Reference | Approved use |
| --- | --- |
| Playboy-style publication architecture | Masthead confidence, editorial hierarchy, recurring departments, strong feature imagery, membership logic, and an authentic inhabited feeling. No adult content, imagery, language, or copied trade dress. |
| Work & Co | Clean product pages, proof-led case studies, and restrained sub-product layouts. |
| Clay | Business service architecture, process explanation, credibility, and consultation conversion. Not the master visual identity. |
| Monocle | Editorial, products, newsletters, events, and membership functioning as one ecosystem. |
| Soho House | Tier and benefit logic. No visual copying. |

Instrument is removed from the positive reference board. Generic AI-agency optics are a negative reference.

## 2. Core Product Thesis

The website itself becomes a DCSE product demonstration.

The primary message is not merely that Sonly Consulting offers strategy, campaigns, AI, and digital products. The site demonstrates how SC thinks, structures, builds, tests, and improves work.

The public DCSE Dossier is a curated publication layer derived from approved SC evidence. It is not a public copy of internal governance, repositories, databases, Tribunal records, prompts, credentials, or control architecture.

Each Dossier entry should answer:

1. What problem or opportunity existed?
2. What approved sources informed the work?
3. What decisions and alternatives were considered?
4. What was designed or built?
5. What evidence was produced?
6. What passed, failed, or changed?
7. What result or present state can be verified?
8. What remains unknown or next?

This creates visible proof without exposing protected mechanics.

### 3.3 The DCSE Dossier

The Dossier is the main proof hub. It may contain these public departments:

- **Build Files:** products, sites, applications, tools, and campaign systems.
- **Campaign Files:** creative briefs, sanitized storyboards, selected assets, and release outcomes.
- **Decision Files:** public-safe options, tradeoffs, selection criteria, and lessons.
- **AI Fieldwork:** how SC uses AI responsibly and where human judgment remains decisive.
- **Before and After:** approved transformations with source and outcome evidence.
- **Working Notes:** philosophy, observations, and forward-looking ideas.

Every entry requires a public-safety review. Internal architecture, private paths, credentials, unreleased client material, and protected-lane content are prohibited.

## 5. PS Design Accounting and Firewall

PS is accounted for as a separate protected architecture, not a public SC subpage.

### 5.1 Protected PS fingerprint

The protected PS experience may use a formal docket-oriented visual system: precise typography, clear chronology, exhibit and issue indexing, restrained color, dense evidence navigation, and federal-court register. This describes design behavior only.

### 5.2 Mandatory isolation

PS must not share:

- public navigation;
- Dossier entries;
- search indexes;
- site maps;
- analytics identities;
- public media libraries;
- forms or lead records;
- AI retrieval collections;
- repositories or public branches;
- metadata, previews, or social cards;
- SC, SS, TI, or general-worker context packets.

No PS facts, names, case references, evidence, strategy, protected DART material, or derived narratives may enter the SC or SS design system.

### 5.3 Public visibility decision

Current safe state: PS has no public doorway from SC.

If DCS later wants a public PS presence, a separate PS-mode declaration, sanitation review, legal-purpose definition, security design, and release decision are required before even a neutral public page is designed.

## 6. Dossier Content and Evidence Standard

Each public Dossier entry requires:

- lane and publication classification;
- approved source list;
- verified public claims;
- sanitized images and media;
- clear distinction among verified result, current state, lesson, and next step;
- rights and release confirmation;
- accessibility text;
- SEO, GEO, and AEO metadata;
- no internal paths, secrets, private identities, protected records, or private architecture;
- final DCS release decision.

Suggested Dossier entry template:

1. Title and one-sentence outcome.
2. Context.
3. Challenge.
4. Inputs.
5. Decisions.
6. Build or creative process.
7. Selected artifacts.
8. Test and evidence.
9. Result or current state.
10. Lessons.
11. Related product.
12. Next action.

## 7. Revised Build Sequence

### Phase 1: Product and ecosystem declaration

Lock SC product families, primary audiences, product boundaries, public Dossier purpose, SS relationship, and PS firewall.

Exit test: every proposed page maps to one lane, one audience purpose, and one approved conversion or engagement outcome.

### Phase 2: Dossier content model

Create the Dossier entry schema, sanitation checklist, evidence standard, department taxonomy, and first five candidate public entries.

Exit test: each candidate can show meaningful work without exposing internal or protected information.

### Phase 3: Sitemap and content architecture

Develop desktop and mobile navigation, page hierarchy, cross-links, search boundaries, and SC-to-SS reveal path.

Exit test: visitors can discover what SC believes, what SC makes, how SC uses AI, and what proof exists within three navigation decisions.

### Phase 4: Three visual directions

Produce exactly three materially different design directions from the same approved brief:

1. **Editorial Dossier:** the strongest publication and masthead expression.
2. **Business Dossier:** clearer business architecture with strong service and consultation pathways.
3. **Living Dossier:** richer motion, recurring features, membership cues, and SS reveal.

All three must avoid generic agency-template optics. DCS selects one direction before code begins.

### Phase 5: Off-site responsive prototype

Build the selected direction outside Wix with a working home page, Dossier index, one Dossier entry, Products index, one product page, SC Philosophy, SC + AI, SS reveal, and Start Here path.

Exit test: desktop and mobile flows pass visual, responsive, accessibility, performance, reduced-motion, conversion, and lane-firewall review.

### Phase 6: Qwen premium media sprint

Use Qwen strategically for campaign imagery, feature art, motion studies, and one short Dossier video. Apply approved brand packs, high-end references, continuity controls, multiple variants, external typography, and artifact QA.

Exit test: accepted assets have clear lineage, rights status, accessibility treatment, technical specifications, and independent quality review.

### Phase 7: Wix staging

Preserve the current live site, capture the before state, map approved prototype components to Wix, implement in controlled staging, and test every supported breakpoint and primary journey.

Exit test: staged output matches the selected direction and passes live browser review without modifying PS systems.

### Phase 8: Release and measurement

Publish only after DCS release approval. Measure Dossier engagement, product-page progression, media engagement, and inquiry actions. Avoid unsupported performance conclusions.
