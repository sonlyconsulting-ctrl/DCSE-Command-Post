## 3. High-End Website Reference Board

These sites are workflow references. They are not copy targets.

| Reference | What SC should study | What SC should avoid copying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument, `https://www.instrument.com/` | One-line premium positioning, work-first storytelling, unified brand, product, website, and campaign services | Excessive motion or portfolio scale that obscures SC's direct offer |
| Work & Co, `https://work.co/` | Tangible business-impact language, disciplined index, case-study proof, strong inquiry capture | Enterprise density and long contact forms |
| Clay, `https://clay.global/` | Clear service architecture, deep case studies, design-system credibility, polished 3D and illustration | Visual effects without corresponding proof or performance control |
| BASIC/DEPT, `https://www.basicagency.com/` | Bold editorial hierarchy, selective work presentation, culture and business-value narrative | Aggressive typography or unconventional navigation that reduces clarity |
| ustwo, `https://ustwo.com/` | Human-centered headline, proof metrics, process clarity, full-stack path from discovery to launch | Broad studio language that weakens SC's specific buyer and next action |

Reference synthesis for SC:

1. Lead with one transformation promise.
2. Show one strong proof story above the fold or immediately after the hero.
3. Package services as a small product ladder, not an undifferentiated list.
4. Use case-study structure: problem, source material, method, deliverables, result, next action.
5. Use motion selectively to support meaning.
6. Make the inquiry path unmistakable and low-friction.
7. Preserve a premium navy, gold, and silver system while improving typography, spacing, photography, and proof.

## 4. Build Sequence

### Phase 1: Product and offer lock

Deliverable: `SC Product Declaration R1`.

Define:

- ideal buyer and highest-value use cases;
- named product tiers;
- required inputs;
- included outputs;
- delivery time;
- revision limit;
- starting price or quote logic;
- rights and publishing responsibility;
- conversion path;
- measurable campaign outcome.

Exit test: a prospective buyer can tell what SC sells, what arrives, how the project starts, and what decision follows.

### Phase 2: Reference and design directions

Deliverable: three materially different responsive homepage or campaign-page directions built from one locked brief.

- Direction A: Executive editorial, inspired by Instrument and BASIC/DEPT.
- Direction B: Proof-led consultancy, inspired by Work & Co and Clay.
- Direction C: Human-centered studio, inspired by ustwo.

ChatGPT Product Design, Claude Design, and Qwen Web Dev may receive the same sanitized brief. Their outputs are comparison inputs. DCS selects the direction and useful components.

Exit test: one direction is selected with a recorded reason and rejected elements.

### Phase 3: Content architecture

Deliverable: source-grounded page and campaign content pack.

Sections:

- hero promise;
- featured proof case;
- product ladder;
- process;
- deliverables;
- ideal clients and use cases;
- trust and evidence;
- FAQ;
- single conversion path.

Claims must be verified against approved SC sources. Unsupported performance or client claims are prohibited.

### Phase 4: Off-site responsive prototype

Deliverable: runnable prototype outside Wix.

Requirements:

- desktop and mobile;
- reusable design tokens and components;
- keyboard navigation and contrast checks;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- image and video performance budgets;
- no live forms, public publishing, or Wix modification;
- implementation-to-reference comparison.

Exit test: selected design and content pass visual, responsive, accessibility, and conversion review.

### Phase 5: Qwen premium asset sprint

Deliverable: governed SC hero image, supporting visual set, and one short campaign clip.

Qwen role:

- image concept generation;
- campaign frames;
- character and scene continuity trials;
- image-to-video or text-to-video candidates;
- alternate compositions and motion studies.

Quality controls:

- approved SC brand pack;
- high-end reference images;
- explicit shot, camera, lighting, palette, aspect ratio, and negative controls;
- minimum three image variants and two video variants for hero assets;
- final typography added outside the generator;
- artifact, motion, anatomy, logo, spelling, caption, audio, rights, and export QA;
- comparison against an alternate generator only for high-value assets.

Exit test: one hero image and one clip meet brand, technical, accessibility, and rights requirements and have receipts.

### Phase 6: Wix staging and controlled implementation

Deliverable: Wix revision plan and staged implementation.

- preserve the current live page until the prototype is accepted;
- record before-state screenshots and page settings;
- map prototype components to Wix capabilities;
- identify manual-transfer limitations;
- implement in a duplicate or controlled Wix revision where available;
- test desktop, tablet, mobile, links, media, metadata, and structured data;
- publish only after the final release decision.

### Phase 7: Campaign launch and measurement

Deliverable: channel package and 30-day measurement record.

- social image and video variants;
- email and social copy;
- SEO, GEO, and AEO metadata;
- analytics events for proof-video play and inquiry action;
- campaign receipt with costs, revisions, assets, and publication references.
