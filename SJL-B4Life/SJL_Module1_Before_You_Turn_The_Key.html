04 Ask SJL™ (Seals Junior Learner) FAQ Console
Direct, structured answers to critical transition questions.
Why does SJL (Seals Junior Learner) teach GPS navigation safety first?
Modern drivers don't just learn to operate steering wheels; they manage screens. Visual distraction is the #1 risk. SJL (Seals Junior Learner) teaches the "3-Second Navigation Check": verify the road is stable and traffic is predictable before glancing at your screen.
Does auto insurance follow the car or the driver?
It follows the car first. If a friend borrows your car with permission and crashes it, your policy pays primary, and your rates will likely rise. Always confirm coverage before handing over your keys.
What is a deductible, and how does it affect me?
A deductible is the out-of-pocket amount you must pay before insurance pays anything. If repairs cost $1,200 and your deductible is $1,000, you pay $1,000 and insurance pays $200. Keeping a clean record helps you lower premiums.
How does driving affect my first job opportunities?
Employers define reliability by shifts started on time. Having a clean driving record and a reliable pre-drive checklist ensures your transportation is secured, directly preserving your professional reputation.
